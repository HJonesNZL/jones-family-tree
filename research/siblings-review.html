Charles Henry Jones’s brothers and sisters

The family’s photograph names six children with Sarah at 18A Arbour Square in 1881. Their occupations help distinguish them from other Londoners named Jones. This review checked the family sheet against the indexed results preserved with Claude’s interrupted research, including the returned record rows. Search previews omit some fields. An indexed birth or marriage quarter gives the registration period. An individual birth date, church, employer or parent requires further evidence. Original family sheet · FreeBMD registration index

Person Findings and limits
Alice Mary Ann Birth registered Oct–Dec 1853, Whitechapel 1c 372. The baptism index search for father Archimedes returns Alice Maryann Jones. The sheet places her with Sarah in 1881 and gives no occupation. An Alice Mary Ann Jones married Edwin Cuthbert Church in the Stepney district in late 1881. Two rival birth registrations, St Luke in 1852 and Poplar in 1854, and the Church household’s later ages leave the bride’s identity unresolved.
Charles Henry Birth registered Apr–Jun 1857, Whitechapel 1c 390. The family photograph explains the March birthday and later registration.
Ernest Augustus Birth registered Oct–Dec 1860, Whitechapel 1c 409. The family calls him a shipbroker’s clerk in 1881. His marriage registered Jan–Mar 1882, Islington 1b 366, is particularly informative. The separate parish marriage index places Archimedes and Katherine Mingay beside Ernest Augustus. Katherine Mingay Smith is on his civil registration page. This explicitly connects the groom to his father and identifies the bride. A possible 1918 marriage in Stepney to Amy Louisa Ansell Argent requires the father's name. No employer or proven children identified.
Grace Emily Birth registered Jan–Mar 1863, St George in the East 1c 482. The baptism search returns the same name. A draper’s assistant in 1881, according to the photograph. Sevenoaks in 1892 and Sheppey in 1908 both have possible marriage registrations. Neither identifies the woman from Arbour Square.
William Edward Joseph Birth registered Jan–Mar 1866 as William E J, St George in the East 1c 443. The sheet calls him a tea merchant’s clerk and names the twins Eric Stephen and Harold Edward. In the 1911 census index the same two boys appear with William Edward Joseph and Ethel Susie, alongside Horace Corby. Ethel Susie Corby and William Edward J Jones share marriage registration Apr–Jun 1898, West Ham 4a 79. The twins and older son’s middle name distinguish this couple. The 1944 probate calendar resolves the rival death entries. William Edward Joseph Jones lived at 80 Porthpean Road and died at St Austell District Hospital on 23 May 1944. The 10 August grant at Llandudno names his widow Ethel Susy Jones. Both distinctive names connect the Cornwall death to the 1911 household. His employer remains unknown. Probate calendar, page 809.
Fanny Elizabeth Birth registered Oct–Dec 1868, St George in the East 1c 433. The sheet lists Fanny E as a scholar. The indexed baptism “Henry Elizabeth Jones” requires an original image before it can be associated with Fanny. Camberwell 1898 and Lambeth 1901 each have marriage candidates; an entry naming her father is still needed.

Alice Mary Ann Jones and the Church marriage

The exact-name birth search returned three near-contemporary London registrations. The family’s known Alice is in Whitechapel, October–December 1853, 1c/372. The other candidates are St Luke, October–December 1852, 1b/546, and Poplar, April–June 1854, 1c/624. It also returned a Newport, Monmouthshire registration in 1855. The 1895 Woolwich death index gives Alice Mary A. Church as age 40. That age allows more than one birth registration depending on the exact dates. The marriage, death and census ages alone cannot establish the link to Archimedes. FreeBMD national index

The Guild of One-Name Studies’ locator identifies Alice Mary Ann Jones and Edwin Cuthbert Church’s December-quarter Stepney entry, 1c/962, as St Dunstan and All Saints. The London Archives catalogue identifies P93/DUN/113, October 1880–December 1882, on film X024/146. The next comparison is the bride’s father and his occupation, her address, and the witnesses against the Arbour Square household. The marriage register image remains unread. Ernest Augustus Jones and Katherine Mingay Smith

The Guild locator identifies the Islington marriage entry 1882 Q1, 1b/366 as St Mary, Upper Street. The archive register is P83/MRY1/1248, April 1881–April 1882, microfilm X085/103. The parish index already associates Archimedes and Katherine Mingay with Ernest. The image could add the exact wedding date, Katherine’s father and the couple’s residences. The civil index provides a separate possible 1918 marriage to Amy Louisa Ansell Argent at Stepney, 1c/454. The public search locates a groom of the right name and age. His father and occupation still need checking. If the suggested St Dunstan venue is correct, its register is P93/DUN/123, film X090/069. Guild marriage locator · Islington register, p. 15 · Stepney register, p. 22

A Catherine Mingay Smith birth was registered October–December 1858 in Plomesgate, Suffolk, 4a/556. The census of 1871 places a Catherine M. Smith, age 12, born in Parham in that district, in Bramley, Surrey, with Susannah Smith and Sarah Jane. The middle initial, age and district make this a lead for Ernest’s wife. A Katherine M. Jones aged 59 died in Wandsworth in the March quarter of 1918, 1d/892. Her age fits the Suffolk birth. A marriage register or household record is still needed to join those records to Ernest. Grace Emily and Fanny Elizabeth

Grace Emily. The 1901 and 1911 census indexes link a Grace Emily Croydon, born about 1863, to Sydney Croydon. The 1911 household also lists Leslie James, John Herbert, Gladys and Margaret Croydon. The December-quarter 1892 Sevenoaks marriage index includes Grace Emily Jones and Sydney Croydon on page 2a/1214. The Guild locator places the entry between Dunton Green St John and Sevenoaks Register Office and leaves the venue open. The full marriage entry is needed to tell whether the bride’s father was Archimedes. 1901 census index · 1911 census index, RG14/20198 · Marriage locator

Fanny Elizabeth. The London marriage index pairs Fanny Elizabeth Jones with Reuben Sharp. The Guild locator identifies their June-quarter 1901 Lambeth record, 1d/708, as St Mary the Less, register P85/MRY2/029, film X070/009. The indexed marriage is returned with an approximate 1882 birth-year filter and drops out with the same filter centered on 1868. The large age discrepancy weighs against the identification. The 1898 Camberwell entry remains unlinked to the family. Lambeth marriage index · Birth-year comparison · Lambeth marriage register, p. 4

William and Ethel’s sons

Horace Corby was registered October–December 1899 in West Ham (4a/268). Harold Edward and Eric Stephen were registered in Romford in January–March 1904 (4a/571 and 4a/572 respectively). The 1911 census index names William Edward Joseph and Ethel Susie as parents of all three. The Contemporary Art Society independently identifies Eric and Harold as twin brothers. 1911 census search · Contemporary Art Society biography

Eric Stephen Jones (1904–1962) studied at Camberwell and the Royal College of Art, specialised in engraving, and taught at Sheffield College of Art from 1931. His wood engraving The Prodigal Son is held at the British Museum. The civil index records his death in Sheffield, January–March 1962, 2d/120, aged 57. The probate calendar gives his exact date as 2 January 1962 and his address as Kirkdale Crescent, Sheffield. These two records resolve the conflicting year in the Contemporary Art Society biography. Museum acquisition record · Probate calendar, p. 295

Harold Edward Jones (1904–1992) was born 22 February 1904. The de Grummond collection, which received his papers directly from him, gives the exact birthday and records his 1933 marriage to Mollie Merry. Both spouses are named on Kensington registration 1a/527, July–September 1933. He illustrated children’s books including Lavender’s Blue and did cartographic work during the Second World War. His Wandsworth death registration gives the same birth date. The probate calendar records his death on 10 June 1992 and a Putney address, resolving the family’s 1992 annotation. de Grummond archival finding aid · National Portrait Gallery · Seven Stories archive · Probate calendar, p. 4752

Mollie Merry (1899–1993) was born on 16 August 1899 according to her death registration. Her birth was registered that quarter in Hampstead, 1a/649. She died on 27 February 1993. Her probate entry gives the same Putney address as Harold’s. The de Grummond archive records that Harold illustrated books for their two daughters. Marriage index · Death index · Probate calendar · de Grummond collection

Horace Corby Jones (1899–1981) was born 29 October 1899 according to his Eastbourne death registration. The exact name and date agree with his birth quarter. His probate entry gives his death as 29 July 1981, an Eastbourne address and a Brighton grant on 1 September. Several civil marriages use the initials Horace C. Jones. None has been tied to him. Eastbourne death index · Probate calendar, p. 4782

Ethel Susy Corby. A birth was registered in the June quarter of 1875 at Mile End Old Town, 1c/522, in the same unusual spelling used in William’s probate entry. A 1961 Wandsworth death aged 86 fits her name and year of birth. It remains a candidate pending a direct link to her family. Birth index · Death index candidate
