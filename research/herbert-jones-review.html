Sarah Herbert of Olney and the Jamaican Jones line

What identifies Sarah

The family sheet records Sarah Herbert’s baptism on 29 January 1826 in Olney and identifies Mary Ann Herbert, baptised there on 3 August 1823, as her elder sister and a witness at Sarah’s marriage to Archimedas Jones on 7 October 1851. Both spouses gave Dock Street addresses. These specific dates provide a way to test the relationship in three separate parish entries. Buckinghamshire’s catalogue lists the Olney parish registers as CBS PR 159/1/1–24. Olney record guide, p. 96

The photograph records the compiler’s account of the entries. It does not show the entries or name Sarah’s parents. Original sheet

Olney’s available FreeREG transcription ends in 1800. A search there cannot decide either sister’s parentage. The Olney & District Historical Society has a separate, free person search covering its local transcriptions. Its 1851 census pages retain the household, occupation, age, birthplace and street fields. The surviving 1851 index gives a specific candidate family. Richard Herbert, born about 1797, is listed as father of Marey Ann (about 1824), Joseph (about 1834), George (about 1837) and William (about 1840). Marey Ann fits the sister named in Sarah’s wedding account, while Joseph fits the Olney-born man in the Sailors’ Home in 1891. Sarah herself is absent from these Olney results. The two matches make Richard a candidate for her father, pending her baptism or marriage register entry. The earlier research located another Olney-born man beside Archimedes in the 1891 Sailors’ Home transcription, Joseph Hubert, aged 57. The birthplace and shared occupation make him worth investigating. His different recorded surname, age and shared workplace cannot establish that he was Sarah’s brother. Look for him in an earlier Olney household, alongside a sister of the right name and age, then compare the parents’ details in the baptism records. How far back the Jones line goes

Joseph Jones is the earliest named person on this branch. The family papers report that Archimedes gave Joseph as his father and described him as a merchant when he married in London in 1851. The family’s estimate of a birth around 1790 remains open. The earliest dated lead for Archimedes is a reported St Elizabeth, Jamaica, baptism on 10 October 1822 giving his birth as 27 September 1815. The civil marriage index lists the couple as Archimedas Jones and Sarah Harbert, Whitechapel district, October–December 1851, volume 2, page 713. The full marriage entry would verify the father’s details. The Jamaican baptism image remains to be checked. Researchers’ account of the marriage and baptism

The detailed 1851 Aldgate census transcription places Archimedes Jones, unmarried, 36, cook, born in Jamaica, among the residents of the Sailors’ Home, Well Street. The Home is schedule 171, where James Laughton is listed as superintendent. The adjoining Destitute Sailors’ Asylum has its own schedule. The 1891 census places the same Jamaica-born man at the Home as a widowed steward, aged 75. Royal Museums Greenwich holds the Home’s wages books from 1873 under SAH/54 and directors’ minutes from 1841 under SAH/1. Archimedes’s death registration

The original General Register Office index gives Archimedes Jones, aged 84, in the Hastings registration district in January–March 1900, volume 2b, page 21. The entry is at the foot of the middle column on printed index page 237. His distinctive name and age fit the 1891 census and the reported 1815 birth date. The full death certificate would give the day, place and informant. Original GRO index scan through FreeBMD · Archimedes’s profile

Who was Joseph Jones?

Page 1 names Joseph Jones as the merchant father reported on Archimedas’s marriage certificate and questions Joseph’s estimated birth year of 1790. The London census identifies Archimedes as born in Jamaica. Neither source identifies Joseph’s estate, Jamaican parish, wife or parents. A researcher reported that the 1822 St Elizabeth baptism gives 27 September 1815 as the birth date and omits the parents. The original register image still needs inspection. The catalogue distinguishes early parish copy registers from later diocesan transcripts. Family photograph · Reported baptism · Register catalogue

Joseph Jones of Old Heathfield, St Elizabeth. UCL dates his recorded ownership to 1809–1817 and cites the 1817 register at T71/165, folios 530–532. The 1820 almanac identifies the proprietor as deceased in its March 1819 returns, and the 1820 register shows possession for Hyman and Judah Cohen, T71/167, folio 144. Those records establish a man of the right name in the parish. His trade and any relationship to Archimedes remain unverified. An October 1813 Royal Gazette notice signed by John Jones of Heathfield shows why the estate name and surname alone are insufficient to identify an individual. Inigo Joseph Jones, Matilda Jones and Margaret McGill. Descendant accounts propose that Inigo and Matilda were children of a Joseph Jones and Margaret McGill. A separate family tree and a source-based study of St Elizabeth also give a Joseph Jones as the father of Elizabeth Ellen Jones, later the wife of Henry Elliott. The study identifies the Elliotts’ New Heathfield coffee estate in Manchester. The returns initially place the Jones property, Old Heathfield, in St Elizabeth. Archimedes’s reported baptism does not name his parents. Compare the adjacent 1822 entries in the Jamaican register and seek separately dated records that name the parents of these other children. Records that could take the line further

  • The full Whitechapel marriage entry, 7 October 1851, under Archimedas Jones and Sarah Harbert, is the first check on Joseph’s name and trade. The original St Elizabeth baptism page could show how Archimedes and any other Jones entries were recorded and whether there is a shared residence or another annotation. The early parish register is on film 1368561, digital folder 004620530. The parish index is on film 1224314. The person-level baptism record requires a FamilySearch sign-in. Whitechapel parish collection P93/MRY1 · Baptism record permalink · Jamaican microfilm list
  • The original wills index available online has no Joseph Jones entry in this period. The enrolled will books, estate inventories and letters of administration are separate sources. Search Jamaica Archives inventory series 1B/11/3 for about 1817–1822. The Old Heathfield transfer from Jones or his representatives to the Cohens could identify an executor or heirs. Check the Island Record Office deed index under both sides. Surviving original wills index · Jamaica Archives holdings · Island Record Office
  • A researcher reported British merchant seaman records for Archimedes. The ticket register, BT 113, and its alphabetical index, BT 114, cover 1845–1854. A matching ticket could give his birthplace, date of birth and evidence of time at sea. His ship and arrival date remain open. Reported seafaring lead · National Archives, series BT 113 and BT 114
